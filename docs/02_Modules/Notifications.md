# Notifications

## Purpose

Defines the Notifications module — the unified delivery system for reminders (`Calendar.md`, `Tasks.md`), Advisor suggestions (`Advisor.md`), and Guardian's passive/urgent findings (`Guardian.md`), including the distinct visual treatment urgent items need and the capped, non-persistent recent-notifications list for MVP.

## Goals

- Route every notification type — reminders, suggestions, Guardian findings, including admin-only alerts — through one general system, rather than maintaining separate delivery mechanisms per source.
- Make urgent items impossible to mistake for routine ones, through clearly distinct visual treatment.
- Keep MVP honest about scope: real-time and in-app only, capped recent list, no lasting history, Android-first — while flagging what a broader release needs.

## User Stories

- As a family member, I want a reminder, a suggestion, and an urgent Guardian alert to all come through the same place, but I want the urgent one to be unmistakably different at a glance.
- As a family member, I want to glance back at my last few notifications during my current session, without expecting them to be kept forever.
- As the Super Admin, I want Guardian's passive and urgent findings to reach me through this same general system, rather than a separate mechanism I have to check elsewhere.

## Features

- **One unified system, all sources.** Event/Task reminders, Advisor suggestions, and Guardian's passive and urgent findings (including the Super Admin-only cases established in `Guardian.md`) all flow through this same general Notification system. Admin-only visibility is achieved through normal recipient targeting (`user_id`) — a Guardian finding addressed to the Super Admin is simply a Notification whose recipient is the Super Admin, not a separate mechanism.
- **Distinct treatment for urgent items.** A notification's `severity` determines its visual treatment — **urgent items display in red** (or otherwise clearly distinct styling), unmistakably different from a routine reminder or suggestion, consistent with `Guardian.md`'s three-tier output model.
- **Capped recent list, MVP.** A user can see their **last 10** notifications in a simple recent list. As new notifications arrive past that cap, the oldest are **removed entirely and not retained anywhere** — no persisted history for MVP, consistent with the real-time-only scope already noted in `Data_Model.md`.
- **Real-time, in-app delivery.** Notifications appear while the app is open, delivered in real time as their triggering event occurs (per `Data_Flow.md`'s notification scenario).
- **Android push for MVP; iOS flagged as a future need.** Since the household currently uses Android exclusively, OS-level push notifications target Android for MVP. iOS PWA push reliability (already flagged as a known limitation in `Technology_Stack.md`) is explicitly noted as something a future, broader release will need to solve — not a concern to resolve now.

## Screen Layout

- **Notification surface (toast/banner style):** appears in real time as notifications are generated, with urgent items visually distinct (e.g., red accent/border) from routine ones.
- **Recent Notifications list:** shows up to the last 10 notifications for the current user, most recent first; older ones simply age out of the list as new ones arrive.
- Tapping a notification tied to a suggestion routes to that suggestion (per `AI_Suggestions.md`'s accept/dismiss/thumbs-down pattern); tapping a reminder routes to the relevant Event/Task; tapping a Guardian finding routes to the relevant Admin Dashboard view (per `Guardian.md`).

## Data Stored

Extends the existing **Notification** entity (`Data_Model.md`) with:

| Field | Notes |
|---|---|
| severity | routine (default) / urgent — determines visual treatment; urgent per `Guardian.md`'s urgent-alert channel |

- The "last 10, then removed entirely" behavior is enforced at the application level (hard delete once a user's notification count exceeds 10), not a soft-delete/archive — consistent with "not remembered anywhere" once a notification ages out.

## AI Responsibilities

- No new AI behavior — this module is the delivery mechanism for notifications already generated by Advisor, Guardian, and the reminder logic in Calendar/Tasks. It doesn't decide *what* to notify about, only *how* it's displayed and capped.

## Permissions

- A notification is only ever visible to its intended recipient (`user_id`), same as any other user-scoped data — this is how admin-only Guardian findings stay admin-only, without a separate permission mechanism.
- No user can view another family member's notifications, including admins viewing a non-scoped user's notifications (per the existing permission model in `Authorization.md`).

## Dependencies

- Builds on `Calendar.md` and `Tasks.md` (reminder triggers), `Advisor.md` and `AI_Suggestions.md` (suggestion notifications), `Guardian.md` (passive/urgent findings and severity model), `Data_Flow.md` (the notification delivery scenario), and `Data_Model.md` (Notification entity, with the new `severity` field).

## Future Enhancements

- **Persistent notification history** (time, text, liked/not), beyond MVP's capped, non-retained recent list — already noted as a future item in `Data_Model.md`.
- **iOS push notification support**, once the household's device mix might include iPhones — explicitly flagged now so it isn't lost, even though it's out of scope while the family is Android-only.
