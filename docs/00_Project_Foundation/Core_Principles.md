# Core Principles

These are the non-negotiable beliefs that guide every product and design decision for Tikas Family Digital Home. Where Cursor_Project_Rules.md governs *how code is written*, this document governs *what the product is allowed to become*.

## 1. Simplicity Over Sophistication

The measure of success is not how advanced the AI becomes, but how naturally it fits into everyday family life. A clever feature that adds friction is a failure, no matter how impressive the technology behind it.

## 2. Trust Is Earned Slowly

The system learns routines and offers suggestions gradually. It does not act boldly before it has demonstrated it can be relied on. Sudden or surprising behavior erodes trust faster than any bug.

## 3. The Family Owns the Home

Family data — documents, photos, memories, conversations — stays on the local network by default. Nothing leaves without explicit, per-action consent. This is a household member's discretion, not a cloud service's convenience.

## 4. Presence Without Intrusion

The AI acts as Assistant, Advisor, and Guardian — but stays in the background until it's needed. It should feel like a dependable member of the household, not a system demanding attention.

## 5. Permissions Are Absolute

Folder permissions and family roles are never bypassed — not by a feature, not by the AI, not for convenience. What a family member can't see, the AI can't show them either.

## 6. Modularity Over Monoliths

Every capability is built as a module that can be understood, tested, and removed on its own. This keeps the system maintainable by a solo developer and prevents any one feature from becoming load-bearing for the whole system.

## 7. Reversibility Over Speed

Destructive actions are reversible by default. It is better for the system to be cautious and correct than fast and unrecoverable.

## 8. Transparency Over Automation

If the AI does something, a family member can ask why and get a real answer. Automation is only trustworthy when it's explainable.

## 9. Reduce Complexity, Don't Chase Features

Tikas Family Digital Home does not try to do everything. It aims to excel at reducing complexity so the family can focus on what matters — and says no to features that don't serve that goal.
