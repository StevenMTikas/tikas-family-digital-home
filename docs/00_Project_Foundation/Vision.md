# Vision

Tikas Family Digital Home is designed to help families organize their lives, protect their knowledge, and simplify daily routines.

## Core Belief

Technology should reduce the complexity of family life, not add to it.

## How We Measure Success

The success of Tikas Family Digital Home will not be measured by how advanced its AI becomes, but by how naturally it becomes part of the family's everyday life.

## The Role of the AI

The AI acts as an **Assistant**, an **Advisor**, and a **Guardian**.

It:

- Answers questions
- Makes thoughtful recommendations
- Protects important information
- Monitors the health of the Digital Home
- Learns routines
- Helps organize memories
- Keeps the family informed

Most importantly, it stays in the background until it's needed.

## What It Should Feel Like

The Digital Home should feel less like software and more like another dependable member of the household.

Rather than trying to do everything, it should excel at reducing complexity and helping the family focus on what matters.
